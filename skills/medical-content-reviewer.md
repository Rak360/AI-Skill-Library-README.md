# Skill: Medical-Aesthetic Content Reviewer

A marketing quality-control aid, not a substitute for a licensed clinician or legal review.

## Review checklist
- Does the content accurately describe the named treatment?
- Are suitability, risks, limitations, variability, and recovery described appropriately when relevant?
- Are guarantees, “permanent,” “100%,” “risk-free,” “instant,” or unsupported duration claims removed?
- Are prices, session counts, device specifications, and credentials verified against approved records?
- Are before/after images genuine, consented, and not misleadingly edited?
- Is patient information protected?
- Is the CTA an invitation to consult rather than a diagnosis or promise?
- Does the draft comply with current platform policies and local regulations?

## Output
1. Status: pass / revise / clinical review required
2. Exact risky wording
3. Why it needs attention
4. Safer replacement wording
5. Facts requiring source verification

## Supreme Balora defaults
Premium, clean, medical, elegant, trustworthy, educational. Preferred colors: burgundy `#7A1F2B` and white. Medical claims must be reviewed by a qualified clinician before publication. Never invent treatment facts or results.
