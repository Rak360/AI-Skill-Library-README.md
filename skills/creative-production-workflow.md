# Skill: Creative Production Workflow

## Goal
Turn a marketing brief into an approved, publish-ready creative with minimal rework.

## Workflow
1. **Brief:** objective, audience, placement, format, language, service/offer, CTA, deadline.
2. **Fact check:** approved details, pricing, claims, references, exclusions.
3. **Concept:** create three distinct angles and select one against the objective.
4. **Script/storyboard:** timed scenes, VO/dialogue, on-screen text, B-roll, transitions.
5. **Asset plan:** footage, image prompts, logos, product/device references, permissions.
6. **Production:** generate/source assets, edit, subtitle, apply brand templates.
7. **Review:** factual accuracy, brand, readability, licensing, accessibility, consent, platform policy.
8. **Approval:** record approver, version, date, clinical/legal sign-off if needed.
9. **Publish:** verify destination, CTA, tracking links, final export.
10. **Learn:** log results and save winning concepts with dates and context.
