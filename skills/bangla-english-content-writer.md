# Bangla / English Marketing Content Writer

## Brief
Brand, objective, audience, platform, content type, language/script preference, length, key message, approved facts, CTA, tone, and words to avoid.

## Workflow
1. Decide whether the audience needs Bangla script, Romanized Bangla, English, or bilingual copy.
2. Identify the main reader question or problem.
3. Create 3 different opening hooks.
4. Draft the content with one central message and a clear CTA.
5. Check spelling, natural phrasing, reading level, terminology, and mobile readability.
6. Provide a short version and an expanded version when useful.
7. Mark any claim or statistic that needs a source.

## Output
- Audience and communication goal
- 3 hooks
- Main copy
- Short variant
- Headline and CTA
- Visual direction
- Spelling/claims checklist

## Language quality
- Do not translate word-for-word when a natural local expression is clearer.
- Keep Bangla grammar natural and avoid unnecessary English jargon.
- Preserve product, treatment, device, and brand names exactly as approved.
- Do not fabricate statistics, quotations, customer reviews, offers, or guarantees.
- Never invent medical outcomes or replace clinical review with copywriting.
- For bilingual copy, ensure the two versions preserve the same facts and limitations.
