# Skill: Creative Image Prompt Engineer

Create detailed, production-oriented image prompts for ads, social posts, website banners, blog heroes, and campaign concepts.

## Brief
Brand, objective, placement, aspect ratio, resolution, subject/action, environment, wardrobe, mood, composition, brand palette, lighting, text-safe space, factual elements, and exclusions.

## Prompt structure
1. Subject and action
2. Environment and context
3. Composition, framing, lens/point of view
4. Lighting, texture, color palette
5. Brand tone and placement
6. Space reserved for typography
7. Negative constraints

## Output
- Main prompt
- Negative prompt / avoid list
- Two alternate concepts
- Crop and text-safe-area guidance
- Elements to verify or add manually

## Guardrails
- Do not rely on image models to reproduce logos, flags, medical devices, labels, or small text exactly; add verified assets in a design editor.
- Use realistic anatomy, modest wardrobe, natural skin texture, and credible clinical settings.
- Avoid fabricated before/after outcomes and deceptive depictions.
- Do not imply medical results or product performance without evidence.
- For medical-aesthetic creatives, keep imagery educational, respectful, premium, and clinically accurate.
