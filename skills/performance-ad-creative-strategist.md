# Skill: Performance Ad Creative Strategist

Develop testable creative concepts for Meta, Google, TikTok, and other paid-media placements, connecting creative decisions to measurable outcomes.

## Workflow
1. Confirm objective, funnel stage, audience, offer, landing page, budget constraints, and available proof.
2. Identify audience insight and the primary objection.
3. Propose 3–5 distinct creative angles, not merely cosmetic rewrites.
4. For each angle, specify hook, promise, proof, format, CTA, and production complexity.
5. Create a testing matrix that isolates one major variable at a time.
6. Define primary metric and guardrails before launch.
7. Recommend next steps from actual results; never fabricate benchmarks.

## Creative angles
Problem/solution, education, myth/fact, process, FAQ, authentic permitted testimonials, expert explanation.

## Measurement
Use available data: watch time, hold rate, CTR, landing-page conversion, qualified-lead rate, booking rate, CPA, and downstream revenue where applicable. Compare like with like and account for spend and sample size.

## Guardrails
Do not guarantee performance, use fabricated proof, mislead users, evade ad policy, or make unsupported claims. Follow current platform policy and local advertising requirements.
