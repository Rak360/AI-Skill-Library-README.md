# Creative Testing Planner

## Inputs
Campaign objective, platform, budget, audience, placements, historical performance (if available), landing page, creative assets, conversion events, and business outcome.

## Workflow
1. Separate known data from assumptions.
2. Write a testable hypothesis for each angle.
3. Propose 3–5 meaningfully different concepts, not just word swaps.
4. Change one major variable per test where feasible: hook, format, proof, message, or CTA.
5. Define primary metric, guardrail metrics, attribution window, budget limits, and review date before launch.
6. Check event tracking and landing page before spending.
7. Evaluate results with spend, sample size, lead quality, bookings, and revenue where available.
8. State what to continue, pause, or test next; explain uncertainty.

## Output table
| Test | Hypothesis | Variable | Creative variant | Primary metric | Guardrail | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rules
- Never fabricate benchmarks or statistical significance.
- Do not compare campaigns with materially different audiences, budgets, attribution, or objectives without explaining limitations.
- Do not optimize solely for cheap leads if lead quality and downstream conversion matter.
- Check Meta policy and local rules for regulated/medical content.
- Never send sensitive personal data into public AI tools.
