# ChatGPT Marketing Apps and Connector Setup

**Reviewed:** 2026-10-24  
**Device:** Android 13 or newer, 8 GB RAM or less, under 5 GB free storage.

This guide distinguishes ChatGPT app availability from account authorization. A listed app is not necessarily installed or connected. Connecting a third-party account always requires the user's own authorization.

## Recommended order

### 1. Canva — design production
- Status checked in ChatGPT app catalog: already installed/available in the user's ChatGPT environment.
- Use: create/edit social posts, resize creatives, remove backgrounds, bulk create where eligible, and check brand consistency.
- Next step: connect/authorize Canva if prompted and confirm the correct Canva workspace.
- Caveat: some AI features use credits; Bulk Create requires an eligible plan. Check the design's final text, logo, crop, and claims before publication.

### 2. ChatGPT Ads Manager — Facebook/Meta ad workflow
- Status checked in ChatGPT app catalog: available, not installed/connected at the time of review.
- Use: inspect campaign performance, draft/preview ads, review account setup and propose supported changes.
- Next step: select the app's connect/install action and authorize only the intended business ad account.
- Safety: read-only analysis does not change campaigns. Creating/updating ads or changing budgets requires explicit confirmation. Confirm account, campaign, destination, budget, and creative before approving anything.

### 3. Metricool — social publishing and analytics
- Status checked in ChatGPT app catalog: available, not installed/connected at the time of review.
- Use: inspect connected-brand analytics, scheduled posts, recommended posting times, and draft/edit scheduled content within plan limits.
- Next step: connect the correct Metricool workspace and review the OAuth permissions carefully.
- Caveat: available actions depend on current integration and plan; do not assume all inbox, ad-management, or publishing features are exposed through every connector route.

### 4. Runway — AI video and creative generation
- Status checked in ChatGPT app catalog: available, not installed/connected at the time of review.
- Use: generate/edit image and video assets, animate stills, create variants and voiceover when supported.
- Next step: connect a Runway account only if video generation is a frequent bottleneck.
- Caveat: check plan credits, pricing, commercial-use terms, and uploaded-media privacy before use. Do not upload patient-identifiable material without appropriate authorization.

### 5. Windsor.ai — cross-channel marketing analytics and supported actions
- Status checked in ChatGPT app catalog: available, not installed/connected at the time of review.
- Use: consolidate data from multiple marketing/analytics sources and, on supported sources, propose account changes or publishing actions.
- Next step: connect only the specific accounts required; begin with read/reporting access where possible.
- Safety: some actions can affect live ad budgets/campaigns or publish posts. Review exact proposed changes and explicitly confirm each live action. Keep a human approval step.

## Low-storage Android rule

The ChatGPT app connectors are cloud integrations and do not require installing desktop software onto the phone, but external accounts and permissions still require user authorization. Avoid installing large local video/image AI applications while free storage remains under 5 GB.

## First acceptance test after connection

- Canva: create one non-sensitive draft and inspect mobile readability and brand consistency.
- Ads Manager: request a read-only 7-day campaign review first; do not change budgets during the initial test.
- Metricool: read one brand's recent analytics and list scheduled posts; do not publish on the first test.
- Runway: generate one non-sensitive test asset and record credits used and output quality.
- Windsor.ai: read a small date-range report first; do not perform write actions during the initial test.

## Privacy and clinical-content guardrails

- Never share passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, API keys, or tokens in chat.
- Connect only the intended business/workspace and review the permission screen.
- Do not upload patient records, private lead sheets, or identifiable clinical images to third-party AI tools without authorization and appropriate safeguards.
- For Supreme Balora Hospital & Aesthetics Care, verify treatment facts, use authentic consented images, avoid guaranteed results, and obtain qualified clinical review before publishing medical claims.
- Publishing and live ad changes must remain behind explicit human approval.
