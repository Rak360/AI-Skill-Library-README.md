# Image Prompt Templates for Marketing

Reusable templates. Replace bracketed fields with verified project details.

## Template A — Premium social creative
**Brief:** [brand], [service], [audience], [platform], [aspect ratio], [approved message], [CTA].

**Prompt:**
Create a polished, premium advertising image for [brand/service]. Show [subject and action] in [environment]. Composition: [framing and placement], with clean negative space at [location] reserved for typography. Lighting: realistic and soft, natural skin texture, credible materials, accurate anatomy, visually uncluttered background. Brand direction: premium, clean, elegant, trustworthy; palette [approved palette]. Output ratio [ratio]. Keep all visible wording out of the generated image so verified text can be typeset later.

**Avoid:** distorted anatomy, synthetic-looking faces, extra fingers, duplicated objects, fake logos, misspelled text, fabricated clinical devices, false before/after effects, exaggerated body features, watermarks, clutter, unapproved medical claims.

**Post-production checks:** add official logo and approved text manually; inspect anatomy and device accuracy; verify crop, contrast, permissions, and licensing.

## Template B — Website/blog hero
Create a wide editorial hero image about [topic] for [brand]. Subject [subject], scene [environment], visual metaphor [metaphor], premium editorial lighting, calm neutral styling with [brand accent], balanced composition, generous copy-safe negative space on [left/right], no embedded text or logos, 16:9 composition. Do not depict outcomes or procedures inaccurately. Confirm mobile crop retains the focal subject.

## Template C — Educational treatment explainer
Create a medically respectful educational visual illustrating [verified treatment concept] in a clean professional setting. Show only [verified device/action] and believable clinical context. Wardrobe is modest and professional; patient identity is not recognizable unless specifically consented and authorized. No graphic imagery, invented equipment, guaranteed outcomes, before/after comparisons, labels, or embedded text. Have a qualified reviewer confirm the depiction before publication.

## Variant checklist
Create variants by changing only one major visual variable at a time:
- Framing: close-up vs. medium shot
- Background: clinic vs. neutral studio
- Subject: clinician-led vs. environment-led
- Copy-safe space: left vs. right

Document each variant and select based on the campaign objective, not personal preference alone.
