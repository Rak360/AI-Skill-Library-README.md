# Short-Form Video Script Template

## Brief
- Brand/service:
- Audience and their question:
- Platform and objective:
- Duration and language:
- Verified facts/source:
- Presenter / voice-over:
- CTA and destination:
- Required disclaimer or clinical review:

## Output format
| Time | Visual / shot | Dialogue / voice-over | On-screen text | Edit / audio notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 sec | Hook shot | [audience-relevant opening] | [short hook] | Start on action; no long intro |
| 3–8 sec | Context | [name the question/problem] | [key context] | Keep cuts purposeful |
| 8–18 sec | Explanation | [clear explanation grounded in approved facts] | [one key point] | Add supporting B-roll |
| 18–25 sec | Trust / next step | [limits, suitability, or useful next step] | [supporting point] | Avoid exaggerated claims |
| 25–30 sec | CTA | [one action] | [CTA] | Hold long enough to read |

Adapt timings to the actual requested duration; the table is a starting structure, not a mandatory 30-second format.

## Deliverables
1. Three hooks with different angles.
2. Final timed script.
3. Shot list and B-roll.
4. Caption, title, and CTA.
5. Claims requiring verification.
6. Production checklist: sound, captions, pronunciation, rights, readability, policy, approvals, tracking.

## Quality control
- No invented outcomes, prices, credentials, testimonials, or urgency.
- Use captions and clear audio; review Bangla and English spelling.
- Make key information understandable without sound.
- For medical topics, obtain qualified clinical review before publication.
